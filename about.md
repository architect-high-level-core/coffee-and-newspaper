<div class="page-body" markdown="1">

**Coffee & Newspaper** is a blog about artificial intelligence – *everything you need to know about AI*. It is run by **Arnold Kahr**.

## What the blog covers

- **Daily AI news briefings:** new models, AI agents, tools, companies and how AI is used in practice.
- **Simple words:** technical terms are explained, so you don't need to be an expert to follow along.
- **Sources linked:** posts link to the original articles, papers and posts they are based on, so you can check them yourself.
- **Read-aloud audio:** most posts have an audio version – press **▶ Listen** to hear the article.

## How the posts are made

Posts are researched with AI assistance from the linked sources, then curated and published by Arnold Kahr. Many posts include a short note on how the sources were checked, and say so when something could not be verified.
