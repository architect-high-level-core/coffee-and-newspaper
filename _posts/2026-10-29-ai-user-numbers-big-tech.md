Almost every big tech company now says its AI assistant has hundreds of millions of users, and some say more than a billion. But the numbers are hard to compare: one company counts weekly users, another monthly users, a third only paying business customers, and some publish nothing at all. Here is what the latest figures say, where they come from, and how Apple and Microsoft fit into the picture.

**How we checked:** we read company announcements and earnings materials from OpenAI, Alphabet (Google), Meta, Microsoft, SpaceX (which owns xAI and Grok) and Apple, Anthropic's IPO filing as reported by CNBC and TechCrunch, the market research firm Counterpoint Research, the app ranking site AICPB, and the developer platform OpenRouter. Company figures are self-reported and not independently audited. Figures from research firms are estimates. Facts we could only find in secondary sources are labelled. We did not use X or Reddit for this post. All dates refer to 2026 unless stated otherwise.

**Key terms:**

- **WAU / MAU:** weekly active users and monthly active users. A weekly count is stricter: someone who uses an app once a month counts as a monthly user, but not as a weekly one. So a WAU figure is not directly comparable with an MAU figure.
- **Self-reported:** the company published the number itself. These numbers are usually not checked by outsiders, and companies choose how to define them.
- **App analytics / panel estimate:** firms such as Sensor Tower or AICPB estimate how many people open a mobile app, based on samples. They often miss web use and use inside other apps.
- **Paid seat:** one paid business licence for one employee.
- **API:** the connection developers use to build an AI model into their own software. API use is billed per token.
- **Token:** a small chunk of text, roughly three quarters of a word.
- **OpenRouter:** a website that lets developers reach many AI models through one connection. Its public rankings show which models developers actually use there, but it is only one platform.
- **S-1:** the document a US company files before going public on the stock market. It must contain audited financial data.

## 1. The big picture: four players above the billion mark

Counterpoint Research estimated that more than 3.8 billion people used AI chatbots and language-model products each month in the first quarter of 2026 (people using several apps can be counted more than once). In that estimate, Meta led with about 1 billion monthly users, ahead of OpenAI (900 million) and Google (750 million). Since then, OpenAI and Google have each reported higher numbers of their own.

User numbers and money are two different things. In the same report, Counterpoint estimated that Anthropic took the largest share of AI revenue in the first quarter (31.4%), just ahead of OpenAI (29.0%), even though Anthropic has far fewer users. The reason: Anthropic earns most of its money from businesses and paying professionals, while most ChatGPT, Gemini and Meta AI users pay nothing. These are research estimates, not company figures.

Original article: [Counterpoint Research: Global LLM Adoption and Revenue Snapshot, Q1 2026](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/Global-LLM-Adoption-and-Revenue-Snapshot)

## 2. OpenAI and ChatGPT: still the largest single app

At its developer conference DevDay on September 29, OpenAI said ChatGPT has 1.2 billion weekly users. On October 7, when it rolled out GPT-6 to all users, OpenAI repeated that "more than 1.2 billion people" use ChatGPT each week. In February the figure was 900 million, so growth has sped up again. One caveat: OpenAI's written DevDay recap described the number as "our collective 1.2B weekly users", so it is not completely clear whether every OpenAI product is included.

In a post on September 8, OpenAI's finance chief Sarah Friar wrote that OpenAI's products reach "more than one billion weekly active users and 2.5 million businesses". The most recent figures on paying users are older: in February, OpenAI reported more than 50 million consumer subscribers and more than 9 million paying business users. We found no newer official update.

Independent app data points the same way. AICPB estimated that the ChatGPT mobile app had about 995 million monthly users in June, by far the most of any AI app.

## 3. Google Gemini: one billion users, and huge API volumes

On October 28, Alphabet reported its third-quarter results. CEO Sundar Pichai said: "The Gemini app is now at one billion users and is the fastest-growing product in Google's history." In July, for the second quarter, Google had reported 950 million monthly active users. For the new figure, Google says "users" without spelling out "monthly", so we treat it as a monthly figure with that small caveat.

Google also gave a number for developer use: its own AI models now process "nearly 32 billion tokens per minute" through its API, 40% more than in the previous quarter and more than four times as much as a year ago. In July, Google said nearly 90% of the Fortune 100 (the 100 largest US companies) use its business product Gemini Enterprise.

Independent estimates for the Gemini app alone are much lower. AICPB put the Gemini mobile app at about 163 million monthly users in June. The gap is large because Google also counts Gemini use on the web and inside Android and other Google products, which app trackers do not fully see.

## 4. Meta AI: a huge reach through WhatsApp and Instagram, but no new number

Meta AI is built into WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and Messenger, which together are used by 3.64 billion people every day. That is why Meta can reach so many people at once. The last official user figure for Meta AI was more than 1 billion monthly users, reported in 2025; Counterpoint also used about 1 billion for the first quarter of 2026.

In its third-quarter call on October 21, Meta did not give a new Meta AI user number. Instead, Mark Zuckerberg said that "engagement per person in Meta AI has more than doubled since April". He also said that more than 10 million people in the US and Canada have set up Muse, Meta's new personal AI agent, within about six weeks of launch, and that about 25% of them use it on any given day.

As with Google, the stand-alone app is much smaller: AICPB estimated about 73 million monthly users for the Meta AI app in June.

## 5. Anthropic and Claude: fewer users, but the biggest business customers

Anthropic, the maker of Claude, published its IPO filing (S-1) on October 26. It does not give a number of consumer users or paid subscribers. Instead, it counts large business customers. As of September 30, Anthropic had:

- more than **11,900** customers spending at least $100,000 a year,
- more than **1,900** customers spending $1 million or more,
- about **250** customers spending $10 million or more.

According to the filing, as reported by TechCrunch, Anthropic's revenue in the first half of 2026 was $16.3 billion, up from $1.2 billion a year earlier. CNBC reported a net loss of $80.7 billion for the same six months, mostly due to accounting effects from convertible notes and derivatives.

How many ordinary people use Claude is only known from estimates. Counterpoint estimated 65 million monthly users in the first quarter; AICPB estimated about 40 million monthly users of the Claude mobile app in June. In March, an Anthropic spokesperson told TechCrunch that paid Claude subscriptions had "more than doubled" in 2026 so far, without giving a total.

## 6. xAI and Grok: 117 million users in the spring, and a fast-growing paid agent

Grok belongs to xAI, which is now part of SpaceX. In its IPO filing, SpaceX said that 117 million people used Grok's AI features each month as of March 31, 2026. According to reports on the filing, this is a subset of about 550 million combined monthly users of X and Grok, and it includes Grok use inside X. We did not read the original filing passage ourselves.

In its third-quarter call on October 20, SpaceX did not give a new Grok user total. President Gwynne Shotwell said Grok Bot, an AI agent launched in August, had 800,000 paying weekly users "earlier this month", more than double the month before. She also said the number of Grok business customers paying more than $1 million a year (including the coding company Cursor, which SpaceX acquired) rose 50% from the previous quarter. AICPB estimated about 68 million monthly users for the Grok mobile app in June.

Original articles: [Forbes: SpaceX's IPO filing says X has 550 million active users](https://www.forbes.com/sites/siladityaray/2026/05/21/x-has-550-million-active-users-groks-spicy-mode-may-cause-reputational-harm-spacex-ipo-filing/) · [The Motley Fool: SpaceX Q3 2026 earnings call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/20/spacex-spcx-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)

## 7. DeepSeek, Perplexity, Mistral and the developer view

**DeepSeek** publishes no user figures. Estimates are similar: Counterpoint put DeepSeek at about 140 million monthly users in the first quarter, and AICPB estimated about 139 million monthly users of the DeepSeek app in June, most of them in China. Among developers, DeepSeek is currently the leader on OpenRouter: in the week beginning October 19, DeepSeek models accounted for 26.5% of all text requests on the platform, ahead of Google (20.1%), OpenAI (18.5%), Z.ai (10.7%) and Anthropic (5.5%). OpenRouter itself points out that this counts requests, not users or spending, and covers only its own platform.

**Perplexity**, the AI search engine, is estimated at about 45 million monthly users by Counterpoint (first quarter) and about 28 million app users by AICPB (June). A figure of about 780 million searches per month in January circulates online, but we only found it in a LinkedIn post, not from Perplexity itself (unverified).

**Mistral AI** from France publishes no current user figures for its chatbot Le Chat, and we found no reliable 2026 number. On OpenRouter, Mistral models had a 2.0% share of requests in the week beginning October 19.

## 9. Apple: no chatbot of its own, but a gatekeeper with partners

Apple does not publish user numbers for Apple Intelligence, and it does not run a chatbot that competes directly with ChatGPT or Gemini. Its role is different: it controls the iPhone, and with it access to well over a billion devices. That makes Apple an important partner for the AI companies.

- **Google Gemini inside Siri.** On January 12, Apple and Google announced a multi-year partnership: the next generation of Apple's own AI models, the "Apple Foundation Models", is based on Google's Gemini models and cloud technology. The two companies said Apple Intelligence will continue to run on Apple devices and on Apple's own "Private Cloud Compute" servers, under Apple's privacy rules. Reports put the value of the deal at about $1 billion a year; neither company has confirmed this (secondary source).
- **Siri AI is rolling out.** On October 26, with the 27.2 software updates, Apple expanded "Siri AI", a fully rebuilt and more conversational Siri, to French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish, in addition to English. It is still labelled beta. Chinese (traditional), Norwegian, Turkish and Vietnamese are due by the end of the year. Siri AI is currently not available in the EU on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, because Apple has not yet reached agreement with the European Commission. Apple also says some server-based features have daily usage limits and that "expanded access to such features will be available for a fee in the future".
- **ChatGPT stays, for now.** ChatGPT has been built into Siri and Apple's writing tools since 2024, and Apple's support pages still describe this integration. According to Bloomberg reports summarised by The Next Web in June, the iOS 27 beta also contains a hidden "Extensions" system that would let users choose between ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, but Apple did not announce it. The same reports say OpenAI was weighing legal steps because it felt Apple had made the ChatGPT integration hard to find. Apple has not confirmed any of this (secondary sources).

## 10. Microsoft: OpenAI's biggest shareholder, a rival, and a cloud for everyone

Microsoft is involved in AI in three ways at once.

**As OpenAI's investor and partner.** Microsoft has invested more than $13 billion in OpenAI since 2019. After OpenAI's restructuring in October 2025, Microsoft's stake was valued at about $135 billion, or roughly 27%. In April 2026, the two companies loosened their deal: Microsoft keeps a licence to OpenAI's technology until 2032, but it is no longer exclusive; OpenAI keeps paying Microsoft a share of its revenue until 2030, but with a total cap; and OpenAI may now sell all its products through other clouds such as Amazon and Google. Azure remains OpenAI's main cloud. Microsoft still books gains and losses from its OpenAI stake: in the quarter to September, they added $207 million to its profit.

**As a seller of its own AI assistant.** Microsoft's AI assistant Copilot is strongest at work. Microsoft reported more than 30 million paid Microsoft 365 Copilot seats in July, and GitHub Copilot for programmers had 50 million users. In its results on October 28, Microsoft did not give a new seat number; according to CNBC, CEO Satya Nadella said the number of people using Microsoft 365 Copilot rose 50% from the previous quarter. The last overall figure for the whole "Copilot family" (more than 150 million monthly users) dates from October 2025. The consumer Copilot app is much smaller: Sensor Tower estimated about 38.5 million monthly users, according to The Next Web in August (secondary). On September 25, Microsoft launched a rebuilt Copilot app that combines chat, task delegation ("Cowork"), app building ("Code") and an always-on agent ("Autopilot").

**As a cloud provider and model builder.** Microsoft's cloud platform Azure grew 48% to $33.2 billion in the quarter, and is now its biggest single business, according to CNBC. Azure sells models from several companies, not just OpenAI: Anthropic's filing names Microsoft Azure, alongside AWS and Google Cloud, as a sales partner for Claude, and Microsoft's Researcher agent works with both OpenAI and Claude models. At the same time, Microsoft is building its own "MAI" models. In October, its Microsoft AI division described MAI-Base-2, a very large model with 2.8 trillion parameters that is still being finished; Microsoft claims it beats every open model it tested (vendor claim, not independently checked). Smaller MAI models for images, voice and coding are already used in Copilot.

## Key takeaways

- **ChatGPT remains the largest AI app:** more than 1.2 billion weekly users, according to OpenAI (September/October 2026).
- **Google and Meta also claim around a billion users,** mostly because their AI is built into Android, Search, WhatsApp and Instagram. Their stand-alone apps are far smaller in independent estimates.
- **Anthropic has far fewer users but earns the most per user:** more than 11,900 business customers spend $100,000 or more a year, and Counterpoint estimates it had the largest share of AI revenue in early 2026.
- **Grok, DeepSeek and Perplexity are in the tens to low hundreds of millions,** mostly based on estimates or older filings. Among developers on OpenRouter, DeepSeek is currently number one.
- **Apple is a gatekeeper, not a chatbot maker:** its new Siri AI runs on models based on Google Gemini, ChatGPT is still built in, and a model picker for rivals is reported but not announced.
- **Microsoft plays on several sides:** about 27% owner of OpenAI, seller of more than 30 million paid Copilot seats, cloud host for OpenAI and Anthropic models, and builder of its own MAI models.
- **Be careful with comparisons:** weekly, monthly, app-only and "users" without a definition are different things, and most figures are published by the companies themselves.
