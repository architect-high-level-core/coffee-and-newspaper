The most valuable chipmaker in the world is, for now, run by its finance chief. On **12 November 2026**, Nvidia announced that co-founder **Jensen Huang** had resigned as president, CEO and board member, and that **Colette Kress**, chief financial officer since 2013, would serve as **interim CEO** ([Nvidia newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition), [SEC Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000082/nvda-20261111.htm)).

Within days, an online campaign began demanding that the board keep the door open for Huang. This article explains who Kress is, what the campaign wants, who supports it and where the board stands. It is a status check as of the morning of **17 November 2026**, the day Nvidia reports quarterly results. It builds on our earlier explainer on [Nvidia’s financing web and Huang’s exit](https://thearchitecthighlevel.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-financing-web-ceo-exit/). For a rival chip boss with a very different story, see our portrait of [AMD CEO Lisa Su](https://thearchitecthighlevel.com/2026/11/17/lisa-su-amd-portrait/).

## The short version

- **New leader (fact):** Colette Kress, 59, is interim CEO and remains CFO until a permanent successor is named. Harvey C. Jones, former CEO of chip-design software firm Synopsys, became Nvidia’s first-ever board chair ([Nvidia](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition)).
- **Important nuance:** Kress is an **interim** CEO, not the permanent successor. The board says it is still selecting a search firm and has not formally engaged candidates ([CNBC, 13 Nov](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-huang-ceo-replacement-search.html)).
- **The campaign (fact, as reported):** a volunteer petition called “Stand with Jensen” has drawn roughly **180,000 signatures**, according to CBS News and CNBC. Elon Musk, Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney and Shopify CEO Tobias Lütke are among the signers named in reports ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/jensen-huang-nvidia-ceo-reinstatement-petition/), [CNBC, 16 Nov](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/nvidia-jensen-huang-return-tech.html)).
- **Shareholders:** Altimeter Capital’s Brad Gerstner and Atreides Management’s Gavin Baker asked the board for a “slow, measured, and deliberate process”, including consideration of “the potential return of Jensen Huang” ([CNBC, 14 Nov](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/14/nvidia-investors-jensen-huang-return-board-letter.html)).
- **The board (reporting, single source):** CBS News, citing a source directly familiar with the board’s thinking, reports that board members are **not interested** in reinstating Huang in any role.

## Who is Colette Kress?

Kress joined Nvidia in **September 2013** after nearly 25 years in finance roles at technology companies, including **Cisco, Microsoft and Texas Instruments** ([Nvidia](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/colette-kress-nvidia-cfo-interim-ceo-candidate.html)). For more than a decade she was the second voice on Nvidia’s earnings calls, presenting results and answering analysts’ questions alongside Huang.

What people say about her (these are **opinions**, quoted by CNBC):

- Analyst Dan Ives of Yorkville Ives: “She’s more than just a CFO. I’d almost view her as more of a COO.” He also said: “Jensen didn’t do anything without her.”
- Daniel Newman of Futurum Group said Kress “deeply understands the business and has shown fierce loyalty to the company.”
- CNBC says nearly a dozen insiders and former co-workers described her as savvy, thoughtful and clear-headed.

Reuters reported that Kress and her finance team had clashed with Huang over large, risky financing deals for Nvidia’s customers, citing two people familiar with the matter ([Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/colette-kress-the-cfo-who-challenged-huangs-bets-is-nvidias-interim-ceo-5023936)). That matters because Nvidia’s guarantees and purchase promises help finance a large part of the AI data-center buildout.

In her first statement as interim CEO, Kress said: “I am committed to building on the extraordinary foundation Jensen created over the last 33 years” and that the focus “remains on serving our customers, partners, developers and shareholders.”

**Key term – interim CEO:** a temporary chief executive who runs the company while the board searches for a permanent one. Ives told CNBC he expects Kress to serve for about a year and the permanent CEO to come from outside. That is **a prediction**, not a plan Nvidia has announced.

## Why Huang left, in brief

According to CNBC and CBS News, Huang resigned after reports of extramarital affairs, including one with a woman U.S. intelligence agencies reportedly suspect of spying for China. Nvidia’s board is continuing an independent investigation into the conduct that led to his departure. This blog focuses on the business and governance consequences, not on private matters.

## The campaign to bring Huang back

### What the petition says

The “Stand with Jensen” website calls itself an independent, volunteer campaign not affiliated with or endorsed by Nvidia or Huang, CBS News reports. Its open letter to the board asks for three things ([standwithjensenhuang.com](https://standwithjensenhuang.com/en/)):

1. **A fair hearing** based on evidence and a chance for Huang to respond.
2. **Transparency** about the process and the interests being weighed.
3. **A path forward:** serious consideration of Huang’s return while the facts are examined independently.

The letter states: “Gratitude does not erase accountability. Fairness makes accountability worthy of trust.”

### How big is it?

- CBS News and CNBC report **more than or nearly 180,000** signatures. The site’s own counter showed a slightly higher number when we checked on 17 November.
- The site says over 9,000 signers are Nvidia employees, roughly one in five staff, according to CBS News. CNBC notes that **affiliations are self-reported and not verified**, so this figure should be treated with caution.

### Who supports it

- **Tech executives:** Musk wrote on X: “A petition is underway to reinstate Jensen and I’m signing it too.” Tim Sweeney, Tobias Lütke and former Uber CEO Travis Kalanick confirmed their support on X, CNBC reports.
- **Investors:** the Gerstner and Baker letter praised Kress as capable but argued that Huang “has earned the right for a deliberate process that avoids any needless rush to replace him before all the facts are known.” Notably, the letter **did not call for his immediate reinstatement**, CNBC reports.
- **Politics:** U.S. President Donald Trump told reporters he “would not mind” Huang returning, then also suggested former Apple CEO Tim Cook as a possible next leader (CBS News, CNBC).
- **Huang himself:** the Financial Times reported that Huang has discussed a possible return with close confidants, as summarised by CNBC and CBS News. Huang has not publicly confirmed this.

## Why a comeback looks unlikely

- **The board:** according to CBS News’ source, board members do not want Huang back in any role.
- **Washington:** U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called for an outside CEO, linking this to questions about whether chips sold to companies in Singapore and elsewhere in Asia ended up in China despite export controls (CBS News).
- **Analysts:** Adam Crisafulli of Vital Knowledge wrote that the “odds of him being reinstated seem quite low”. Dan Ives called comeback talk “ridiculous in our view”.
- **Prediction markets:** CBS News reported that traders on Kalshi priced a Huang return at about **10%**. Prediction markets reflect bettors’ views, not facts.

## FAQ

**Is Colette Kress Nvidia’s new permanent CEO?**
No. She is interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent successor, and she continues as CFO.

**Can a petition force Nvidia to rehire Huang?**
No. Choosing the CEO is the board’s decision. Shareholders can apply pressure, for example through letters or votes on directors, but an online petition has no legal power.

**Did major investors demand Huang’s return?**
Not exactly. Two shareholders asked the board to consider all options, including his return, and to avoid rushing.

**Will this affect AI chip supply?**
The board said it expects operations, customer commitments and the product roadmap to continue without interruption. Whether financing deals change under new leadership is an open question.
